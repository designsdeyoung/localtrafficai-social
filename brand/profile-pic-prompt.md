# Profile Picture: Higgsfield Image Prompt

Goal: a clean, logo-style avatar that reads as "LTA / Local Traffic AI" even
when Instagram shrinks it to a 110px circle. No photographic clutter, no faces,
no fine text. One bold mark on a solid field.

## Brand facts to keep consistent

- Primary color: emerald green `#22c55e`
- Accent: electric blue `#3b82f6`
- Dark base: deep navy `#0a1020`
- Light base: off-white `#fafafa`
- Personality: sharp, modern, practical. Reads like a tool, not a clip-art logo.

## Model

Use **nano_banana_2** (unlimited on the plan, crisp for graphic/logo work).
Aspect ratio `1:1`. Generate 4, pick the cleanest at small size.

---

## Prompt (copy-paste)

```
Minimal flat vector logo mark for a tech brand called "Local Traffic AI",
designed to live inside a circle as a social media avatar. A bold geometric
monogram combining the letters L, T, A into a single confident mark, OR a
clean map-pin shape with a subtle upward signal/traffic arrow inside it.
Solid deep navy background (#0a1020). The mark in vivid emerald green (#22c55e) with
one thin electric blue (#3b82f6) accent stroke. Thick strokes, high contrast, generous
negative space, perfectly centered, no gradients, no drop shadows, no 3D, no
photographic elements, no small text, no taglines. Sharp and legible when
scaled down to 110 pixels. Modern fintech/SaaS aesthetic, balanced and
geometric, single focal mark.
```

## Negative / avoid (paste into the negative prompt field if shown)

```
realistic photo, human face, busy background, gradient mesh, drop shadow,
bevel, 3D render, multiple objects, paragraph text, small lettering, watermark,
clutter, skeuomorphism, stock-photo look
```

---

## Two safe directions if you want variants

1. **Monogram lockup**, "LTA" stacked or interlocked, emerald green on black. Most
   brand-recognizable, scales best.
2. **Map-pin + signal**, a pin (local) with a rising bar/arrow inside (traffic
   growth). More illustrative of what the product does.

Generate both, then test the screenshot trick below before committing.

## The 110px test (do this before you upload)

1. Export the candidate at 1:1.
2. Drop it on a phone, shrink the preview thumbnail.
3. If you can't tell what it is from across the room, it fails. Pick the one
   with the boldest single shape and the most empty space around it.

## Upload notes

- Instagram crops to a circle, keep the mark inside the center ~80% so nothing
  important gets clipped at the edges.
- Upload at least 320x320; 1080x1080 is safer for future-proofing.
